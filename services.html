Common questions

Before you ask

Anything else? Send us a note — we reply within one business day.

Do you replace our photographer?

No — we're the layer alongside them. Formal portraits and albums stay with your photographer; we cover the candid, social-first record of the day.

What exactly arrives in 24 hours?

Edited vertical clips, story-ready sets, and candid photos, delivered electronically. Larger recap edits follow when a package includes them.

Do you travel outside Tampa?

Tampa Bay is home — St. Pete, Clearwater, and Sarasota included. For events beyond the Bay, ask; we often say yes.

How far ahead should we book?

Weddings and holiday weekends fill first — two to three months ahead is safe. Smaller celebrations can often be covered with a few weeks' notice.
