How a celebration becomes a story

Three timelines that show what working with us actually looks like — from the first message to the moment the clips land in your inbox.

Story 01 · Wedding

The wedding that trended in its own group chat

The couple already had a photographer and videographer booked. What they didn't have was anyone catching the day as it felt — so we covered the getting-ready suite, filmed the ceremony from the guest's-eye view, and stayed through the last dance.

By Sunday morning the couple had a reel, a story set, and forty candids. Their families reposted the clips before the honeymoon flight boarded.

The host who finally made it into her own photos

A milestone birthday, thirty guests, and a host who usually spends her parties holding the phone. This time, she handed the documenting to us — decor details before the doors opened, arrivals, the cake moment from two angles, and the dancing after.
